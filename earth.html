Our Blue Planet

Earth is the third planet from the Sun. From space, its oceans make it look like a blue marble.

A few Earth facts

  • One trip around the Sun takes about 365 days.
  • Earth has one Moon.
  • Our atmosphere contains the oxygen we breathe.
